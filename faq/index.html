Escape the Beaver FAQ

Current answers from the official page, official badges, and cross-checked gameplay.

8 answersLast verified August 10, 2026
01

What is the goal of Escape the Beaver?

The official description says to survive, hide, and escape an abandoned gas station that closed for an unknown reason. Two independent full-playthrough videos also show players searching for vehicle parts as they work toward escape.

02

What are Bucks used for?

Bucks can buy Special Weapons and revive other players without Robux. The official description says weapons can be used against the Beaver or other players, but it does not publish verified names, prices, or damage values.

03

How do I earn the “First Win!” badge?

Complete the game. For a first serious run, prioritize the normal escape objective instead of also trying to collect all 10 notes.

04

How many notes are needed for the Heartbreak Badge?

The Heartbreak Badge requires all 10 notes on the map. The official badge text does not confirm whether they must be found in a single run, so this guide does not make that claim.

05

How many current badges are there?

The official Badge API listed five badges when checked on August 10, 2026: Welcome Badge, First Win!, Heartbreak Badge, Complete Nightmare Mode, and Complete Bucky Doom!

06

How many Bucky Doom rounds must I complete?

Complete all 12 rounds for the “Complete Bucky Doom!” badge. Its official description also promises a free invisible skin, but does not explain individual round tasks or how to equip the reward.

07

Does Nightmare Mode have a completion badge?

Yes. Complete Nightmare Mode awards the Complete Nightmare Mode badge. Current official badge data does not explain the mode's detailed rules.

08

Is this an official Escape the Beaver website?

No. This is an independent player guide. The game itself is described as fan-made and a tribute to Buc-ee's; that does not mean this guide or the game is officially authorized by Buc-ee's or Roblox.
